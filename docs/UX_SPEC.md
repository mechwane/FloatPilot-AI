# UX Specification — FloatPilot AI

## Phase 1 design direction
Original, calm, premium financial-operations interface using deep navy, clear operational blue, cool neutral surfaces and restrained amber risk cues. Avoid third-party banking identity, copied interfaces, excessive gradients, glassmorphism, confetti and decorative AI visualisations.

## Public routes
- `/`: product explanation, how-it-works steps, static scenario teasers, safety statement and demo CTA labelled **Explore Interactive Demo**.
- `/demo`: fixed synthetic scenarios with local-only selection, visible persistent disclosure and clear decision-support/no-funds-moved wording.
- `/agent`, `/operations`, `/admin`, `/judge`: minimal informational future-phase pages only; no account controls, dashboards, forms or active operational functions.

## Interaction and accessibility
Use semantic headings, landmarks, buttons and links; visible keyboard focus; a skip-to-content link; accessible labels and live announcement for changed scenario detail; support reduced motion. Scenario controls must work by keyboard and locally without network requests. Layout target widths: 390px, 768px, 1280px and 1440px.

## Safety language
All figures, names, agents and locations are fictional and illustrative. The product is a decision-support concept, not a live forecast or financial service. Human review remains necessary. No funds move automatically or otherwise in Phase 1.
